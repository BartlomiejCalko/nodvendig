# NØDVENDIG WEBSITE — PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

**Project:** Nødvendig Holding AS Corporate Website
**Version:** 1.0
**Market:** Norway / Nordic Region
**Primary industry:** Data Center Construction / Critical Infrastructure
**Primary language:** English
**Framework:** Next.js / TypeScript
**Document purpose:** Product definition and implementation requirements

---

# 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

The Nødvendig website is a premium B2B corporate website positioning **Nødvendig Holding AS** as a construction and execution partner for data center projects in Norway and, increasingly, across the Nordic region.

The website must communicate one clear market position:

**Nødvendig builds and transforms the physical environments required by modern data centers and critical infrastructure.**

The website must not present the company as a general contractor for residential construction or small renovation projects.

The historical focus of the company is irrelevant to the new brand positioning and should not be communicated.

The website represents where Nødvendig is going.

---

# 2. BUSINESS CONTEXT

Nødvendig has developed from a general construction organisation into a company increasingly focused on data center environments.

The company has participated in construction and repair work in several data center environments in Norway and is currently heavily involved in a major transformation project in Tydal.

The Tydal project involves the physical conversion of an existing cryptocurrency mining data center into infrastructure intended for AI and high-performance computing workloads.

Nødvendig works alongside companies responsible for critical infrastructure and contributes:

* construction execution
* site organisation
* workforce
* structural works
* demolition
* reconstruction
* building modifications
* technical-space preparation
* coordination
* problem solving
* on-site management

This intersection between **construction execution** and **critical infrastructure environments** is the central positioning opportunity for the company.

---

# 3. PRODUCT VISION

Create a digital presence that makes Nødvendig appear immediately credible within the professional data center construction market.

The website should communicate:

**“This company understands complex construction environments and knows how to deliver physical work around critical infrastructure.”**

Within approximately five seconds, a visitor should understand:

1. Nødvendig works in data centers.
2. Nødvendig physically builds and transforms facilities.
3. Nødvendig operates in Norway.
4. Nødvendig can support complex mission-critical projects.

# 8. BUSINESS GOALS

The website should:

1. Establish Nødvendig as a data center construction company.
2. Create credibility with larger contractors and data center operators.
3. Generate inbound project enquiries.
4. Support business-development conversations.
5. Provide a professional URL that can be shared during tenders and negotiations.
6. Help recruit skilled workers and site leaders.
7. Create a platform for future project case studies.
8. Support expansion from Norway into the broader Nordic market.

---

# 9. NON-GOALS

The website does not initially need:

* e-commerce
* customer accounts
* complex dashboards
* multilingual CMS
* extensive news functionality
* online quoting
* customer portal
* advanced CMS
* interactive project management functionality

The first version should prioritize brand, credibility, storytelling, projects and lead generation.

---

# 10. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

## Clarity over volume

Use fewer words, but make them meaningful.

## Credibility over hype

Never exaggerate Nødvendig's experience.

## Real work over stock imagery

Actual construction photography should eventually dominate.

## Engineering over decoration

Graphics should come from architectural and construction language.

## Motion with purpose

Animations should explain, reveal or guide.

## Premium without luxury

The website should feel expensive and professionally designed, but industrial rather than fashionable.

---

# 11. VISUAL DIRECTION

The experience should combine two broad reference directions.

### Engineering precision

Inspired by the underlying principles visible in premium industrial websites such as Podium Automation:

* grid-based compositions
* technical diagrams
* large typography
* strong structure
* restrained colors
* industrial confidence

### Digital motion and storytelling

Inspired by premium creative studio websites such as Synapser Studio:

* fluid page transitions
* immersive scrolling
* expressive typography
* motion-led storytelling
* visual pacing
* unusual but controlled compositions

Nødvendig should combine these into its own original identity.

Recommended balance:

**70% engineering / infrastructure**

**30% digital experience / motion**

# 17. MOTION REQUIREMENTS

Motion should be used extensively enough to establish premium quality, but never reduce clarity.

Required motion concepts:

* subtle text reveal
* line-drawing animations
* SVG path animations
* viewport-triggered transitions
* smooth section transitions
* image reveals
* light parallax
* sticky storytelling
* smooth navigation transitions

Possible interaction areas:

* data center technical diagram
* BUILD / TRANSFORM / DELIVER
* Tydal transformation
* project photography
* technical labels

Motion should feel:

**precise**

not:

**playful**

Avoid:

* bouncing
* exaggerated spring effects
* excessive scale effects
* visual noise
* scroll-jacking

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

* minimal layout shift
* optimized images
* lightweight SVG
* limited client-side JavaScript
